Copy some text anywhere (Ctrl/⌘ + C), then come back to this tab. The clipboard is read automatically, and emails, phone numbers, URLs, dates, amounts, Key: Value lines and your custom fields land in this table.

You can also press Ctrl/⌘ + V anywhere on this page.

One copy can create many rows: every contact in a list, every line of a spreadsheet.
